# Phone checklist

What the agent cannot check, because it has no phone: how the app feels in
a hand. Each link opens straight on the thing to look at. Touch the screen
once to let a held view fly on.

Open the site first, and check the build under the planet's name matches
the latest deploy — if it does not, pull down to reload, or close and
reopen the installed app.

Base: <https://andreibautin.github.io/planet-generator/>

## The basics

- [ ] **First load**: from tapping the link to a planet on screen, roughly
      how many seconds? On mobile data and on Wi-Fi if you can.
- [ ] **Orbit**: spin the planet with a finger. Smooth, or does it stutter?
- [ ] **Fly**: press Fly and glide for a minute. Smooth? Does the ground pop
      in, or flicker anywhere?
- [ ] **Heat and battery**: after five minutes of flying, is the phone warm?
      Rough battery drop?
- [ ] **Sound**: press the speaker. Wind while gliding, surf along a coast.
- [ ] **Install**: Add to Home Screen, open it from there. Full screen?

## Worth sending back

- The phone's model, and whether anything above stuttered, flickered or
  looked wrong — a screenshot with the build line in it is the most useful
  thing of all.
- The share sheet: make a **Postcard** and a **Clip** and try to share
  each. The agent's browser cannot open a share sheet, so these have never
  been seen working on a phone.
